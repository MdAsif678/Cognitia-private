How it works

What happens when a site asks you to sign.

  1. 01 · INTERCEPT

    The request is held

    The extension sits between the website and your wallet. The wallet popup does not open yet.

  2. 02 · SIMULATE

    It runs on a private fork

    An isolated Anvil fork of mainnet or Sepolia, pinned to a verified block, executes the exact request. Nothing reaches the real chain.

  3. 03 · ASSESS

    Effects become a verdict

    Calldata is decoded, before/after balances and approvals are diffed, and addresses, bytecode and domains are checked against a drainer database.

  4. 04 · DECIDE

    You choose

    Reject cancels the request (code 4001). Continue forwards the exact reviewed payload to your wallet, once.

The report

A verdict, not a wall of hex.

Each report starts with a severity and a one-line explanation, then shows the exact state changes: what you lose now, what an attacker could take next, and what was checked. If analysis fails, the report says so. A failure is never shown as safe.

  • Immediate changes vs. what a signature allows later
  • Native, ERC-20, ERC-721 and ERC-1155 balances, allowances and operators
  • Coverage and assumptions stated openly
  • Downloadable JSON evidence for every verdict

What it catches

The tricks drainers actually use.

  • High

    Unlimited token approvals

    approve(spender, max) to an unknown or unverified address, with the amount an attacker could then take.

  • High

    Approve-all on NFTs

    setApprovalForAll on ERC-721 and ERC-1155 collections, listing every token now exposed.

  • Critical

    Hidden outflows

    Functions named claim or mint that quietly transferFrom your assets to someone else.

  • High

    Off-chain permits

    ERC-2612 and Permit2 signatures that grant spending rights without a transaction.

  • Medium

    Unknown calls and delegatecalls

    Unrecognised selectors and delegatecalls into other code are flagged, never assumed safe.

  • Critical

    Known drainers and phishing sites

    Documented drainer addresses, runtime bytecode hashes and phishing domains, with sources.

Honest limits

What it does not promise.

  • Interception wraps injected wallet providers. Direct RPC transports, WalletConnect and other frames can bypass it.
  • A signature is simulated as what it would authorise. That does not prove the signature itself is valid.
  • The drainer database is curated, not a live feed. No match is not a safety guarantee.
  • Asset discovery is limited to what calldata, logs and traces reveal. It is not a full wallet inventory.
